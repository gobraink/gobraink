GOBRA I.N.K.M. HIGH SCHOOL was established on 22nd of January in 1928, This venerable institution is under the administrative stewardship of the Govt. of West Bengal, specially managed by the Department of School Education(DSE).

S.I.(Digha Circle)

It is nestled in Gobra , a severe rural locale within RAMNAGAR -1 block of Purba Medinipur district in West Bengal. Notably, the campus boats seamless, all-weather accessibility , ensuring uninterrupted educational pursuits regardless of metrological conditions.

The comprehensive academic structure caters to scholars from Grades V to X (under the aegis of the WBBSE) and Grades XI to XII (Under the auspices of the WBCHSE). auspices of the WBCHSE).
i) Co-Educational Dynamics: The institution proudly champions a co-educational framework.
ii) Early Childhood Infrastructure: It operates independently without an attached pre-primary division.
iii) Medium of Instruction: Bengali serves as the primary linguistic vehicle for scholastic discourse and instruction.
iv) Academic Calendar: The annual academic session commences systematically in January.

The school occupies government building featuring 20 excellent classrooms for instructional purpose , 2 auxiliary rooms for non-teaching activities and a separate room for Headmaster and teachers. Enclosed by a robust pucca boundary wall , the campus is fully electrified. Crucially, the primary source of drinking water is a fully functional tap water system. Sanitation is extremely strong, maintaining 11 functional toilets for boys and 5 functional toilets for girl, all in perfect working order. The campus also includes a school play ground, a dedicated mid-day-meal dining hall and a residential hostel-building. Due to the favorable layout, the school does not need access ramps for disabled children to enter classroom.

The school features a well-equipped library containing approximately ,1170 books.

TThe school has 18 fully functional computers dedicated to teaching and learning. Additionally ,it features a Computer Aided Learning Lab. as well as an ICT Lab. .
